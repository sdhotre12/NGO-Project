Understanding the Signals: Early Signs of Abuse

Abuse doesn’t always start with violence — it often begins with subtle behaviors that slowly gain control over a person’s life. Recognizing these early signals can prevent emotional, physical, and psychological harm before it escalates.

💠 Early Warning Signs

  • Excessive jealousy or possessiveness
  • Constant checking or monitoring (calls, messages, location)
  • Isolation from friends, family, or support networks
  • Criticism, insults, or humiliation disguised as “jokes”
  • Sudden mood swings or anger over small issues
  • Financial control, limiting access to money or personal choices
  • Pressure or guilt to do things against one’s will

💠 Why Awareness Matters

When survivors — or their loved ones — recognize these early signs, they can take steps to set boundaries, seek help, or safely exit a harmful situation. Awareness builds strength, and early action can save lives.
