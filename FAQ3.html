Safety & Complaint Processing FAQs

Information regarding filing and processing of safety complaints.

A person intending to file a complaint before the Commission for women may do so by making a complaint on the official website of the Commission https://ncwapps.nic.in under the section of Register Online Complaints. One can also send a written application containing all the important details (along with supporting documents, if any) through post or by hand.

Any case involving deprivation of women’s right or harassment of women in India can be registered with the Commission for Women. The complaint should disclose complete details of the matter with specific relief/intervention sought from the Commission for Women.

The Commission entertains a wide range of complaints, including:

  • Abduction
  • Acid Attack
  • Causing Miscarriage
  • Criminal Intimidation/Assault
  • Cybercrimes Against Women
  • Denial Of Maternity Benefits to Women
  • Domestic Violence
  • Dowry Harassment
  • Dowry Death
  • Gender Discrimination, Including Equal Right to Education & Work
  • Murder
  • Attempt To Murder
  • Outraging Modesty of Women/Molestation
  • Police Apathy Against Women
  • Polygamy/Bigamy
  • Triple Talaq
  • Rape
  • Request For Free Legal Aid to Women
  • Right To Exercise Choice in Marriage
  • Honor Killing
  • Sex Selective Abortion; Female Foeticide / Amniocentesis
  • Sexual Assault
  • Sexual Harassment
  • Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace
  • Stalking
  • Voyeurism
  • Trafficking
  • Forced Prostitution of Women
  • Dehumanizing And Stigmatization of Women

The following categories of complaints are liable to be summarily dismissed:

  • Complaints illegible or vague, anonymous or pseudonymous.
  • If the matter relates to a civil dispute between the parties such as contractual rights or obligations.
  • The issue raised relates to service matters not involving any deprivation of women’s rights.
  • The issue raised relates to labour/ industrial disputes not involving any deprivation of women’s rights.
  • Matter is sub judice in a Court/ Tribunal.
  • Matter is pending before a State Commission.
  • Matter has already been decided by the Commission.
  • Matter is outside the purview of the Commission for Women on any other ground.

An acknowledgment along with **complaint number, login ID and password** is sent to the complainant upon receipt of the complaint if it has been accepted. In the event of the complaint being rejected, the same shall be communicated to the complainant at the earliest. You can also check telephonically or by personally visiting the Commission for Women.

If your complaint is accepted, you will be allotted a complaint number with login ID and password which can be used to track the status of your complaint on the website of the Commission. You can also check the same telephonically or by personally visiting the Commission for Women.

For complaint related queries dial the **24x7 Women Helpline Number: 7827170170**.

You can contact us anytime between **9:00 AM – 5:30 PM** on **Working Days (Monday to Friday)**. You can also send details through email at any time.

You should provide the following details:

  • Contact details of the complainant and the respondent (if available)
  • Brief description of the incident
  • Details of remedies already exhausted
  • Supporting documents (if any)

On receipt of a complaint, the commission:

  • Scrutinizes the complaint as per the mandate.
  • Mandated complaints are registered and a case number is allotted.
  • Non-mandated complaints are summarily dismissed upon intimation.

Following actions are mainly taken to redress the grievances:

  • Expediting/monitoring police investigation.
  • Monitor proper implementation of statutory provisions.
  • Resolving the issues through mediation/counseling.
  • For serious crimes, the Commission forms an Inquiry Committee which further examines various aspects of the case.
