📜 Laws Related to Women

1️⃣ The Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961

This law prohibits giving, taking, or demanding dowry before, during, or after marriage. It protects women from harassment or violence related to dowry. Violation can lead to imprisonment and fines.

Read Official Law

2️⃣ Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005

This act provides protection to women from physical, sexual, verbal, emotional, and economic abuse within households. It empowers women to seek protection orders and residence rights.

Read on WCD Website

3️⃣ Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013

This act ensures a safe and respectful working environment for women. It mandates Internal Complaints Committees in every organization to address harassment cases.

Know More

4️⃣ The Maternity Benefit Act, 1961

This act ensures paid maternity leave and job protection for women during maternity. It allows 26 weeks of paid leave for working mothers and also covers adoption and surrogacy cases.

Official PDF

5️⃣ The Equal Remuneration Act, 1976

This act ensures that men and women get equal pay for equal work. It prohibits discrimination in recruitment and employment on gender basis.

Read Full Act

6️⃣ The Indecent Representation of Women Act, 1986

This law prohibits indecent depiction of women in advertisements, publications, and electronic media. It aims to preserve the dignity of women in all forms of communication.
