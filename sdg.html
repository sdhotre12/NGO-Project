💡 Basic Safety Tips: Awareness is Key

Be aware of your surroundings and always trust your intuition. If a situation feels wrong, it probably is—leave immediately.

Stay in **well-lit and populated areas**, especially at night. If possible, avoid shortcuts through isolated or dark places.

Carry a **whistle, pepper spray, or personal alarm** (check local laws). Have it easily accessible in your hand or outer pocket, not buried in a bag.

Inform a **trusted friend or family member** about your itinerary and estimated time of arrival. Use a live location sharing feature if available.

Avoid distractions like headphones or constantly looking at your phone when walking alone. **Be present** and vigilant.

👊 Simple Self Defence Moves: For Quick Escape

The primary goal is to create an opportunity to **escape**, not to win a fight. Target vulnerable areas and run!

  • Palm Strike
    +
    **Target:** Nose or Chin. Use the heel of your palm to strike upward and forcefully into the attacker’s nose or chin. This causes pain and disorientation, allowing you to run.
  • Groin Kick
    +
    **Target:** It is a highly effective way to get an attacker to release you. The groin is one of the most sensitive areas for men and hence a good body part to attack. You can use your knees to hit your attacker or use a front kick to the groin.
  • Knee Strike
    +
    **Target:** Groin or Midsection. Bring your knee sharply up. This is highly effective at close range for causing pain and briefly immobilizing the attacker.
  • Straight Punch
    +
    **Target:** A punch is the easiest way to get someone out of your personal space. But it is also important to remember to throw a proper punch. Else, you could end up with broken fingers.
  • Eye strike
    +
    **Target:** A very simple self-defence move that can be used in any situation. An eye strike forces your attacker to loosen their grip on you and allows you to use a more forceful move or run away altogether. Since the eye is a sensitive area, striking this part renders the attacker helpless.
  • Hammer fist strike
    +
    **Target:** A hammer fist is a versatile strike and also a fairly easy self-defence move for women. You can throw a punch in any direction and from any position. Standing, sitting or even if you are lying down, you can strike a hammer fist. You can use a hammer fist to attack the throat, nose, jaw, stomach, or groin.

🚨 Emergency Contacts: Know Them By Heart

In an emergency, dial these numbers immediately or use the SOS button on your phone.

  • Police: 100
  • Women’s Helpline: 1091
  • Ambulance/Fire: 102

✅ Additional Safety Reminders

  • Keep your phone **charged** and accessible at all times, with an emergency contact on speed dial.
  • Attend **self-defence workshops** or classes offered by local NGOs/police for hands-on experience.
  • Use security apps that share your live location with trusted contacts and have a panic button.
  • Trust your gut feeling and **report suspicious behavior** immediately to authorities or security personnel.
