Annual Report 2021-2022

Empowering & Protecting Girls — One Step at a Time

About Us

Girl Safety NGO is dedicated to creating a safer environment for girls and women in our community. Through awareness campaigns, education, and direct support services, we aim to reduce violence and promote equality.

Highlights of 2021-2022

  • Organized over 50 safety awareness workshops across schools and colleges.
  • Supported 120+ survivors of domestic and street violence with counseling and legal assistance.
  • Launched our online safety platform, reaching over 10,000 users nationwide.
  • Collaborated with local authorities to strengthen community policing initiatives.
  • Published guides and educational resources on emotional and digital safety for girls.

Programs & Initiatives

  • Safe Streets Campaign: Conducted street patrol awareness and self-defense sessions.
  • Online Safety Initiative: Workshops and resources on digital harassment and cyberbullying.
  • Community Support Groups: Monthly sessions providing emotional support and peer mentoring.
  • Legal Aid Program: Assistance and guidance for survivors seeking justice.
