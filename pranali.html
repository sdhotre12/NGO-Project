Problems Faced by Young Women in India

Harassment

Many women face harassment in public spaces, workplaces, and online platforms, impacting their mental health and safety.

Lack of Education

In some areas, girls are denied proper education, which restricts their opportunities and independence.

Workplace Inequality

Young women struggle with unequal pay, lack of opportunities, and workplace discrimination.

Health Issues

Poor access to healthcare, nutrition, and hygiene continues to be a major challenge for young women.
