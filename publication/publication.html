Girl's Safety

Welcome to our girl’s safety publication, a trusted space for expert insights, practical advice, and the latest thinking on personal safety. At Girl Safety & Welfare NGO, our goal is to create a society where every girl feels safe, valued, and empowered. Our publications represent the voice of change — bringing together research, real-life stories, and actionable insights that shed light on issues affecting girls today.

Through our reports, articles, and awareness materials, we strive to:

• Highlight challenges faced by girls and women in different environments — at home, on the streets, in schools, online, and at workplaces.
• Share the impact of our safety programs, awareness campaigns, and training initiatives.
• Educate communities about self-defense, mental health, cyber safety, and women’s rights.
• Promote collaboration between government bodies, educators, parents, and youth to build safer spaces.

Each publication reflects our belief that awareness brings change — and informed individuals can create a brighter, safer future for the next generation.
