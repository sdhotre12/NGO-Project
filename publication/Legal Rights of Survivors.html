Legal Rights of Survivors – What You Should Know

Every girl and woman has the right to live free from violence, abuse, and harassment. Knowing your legal rights can empower you to seek help and protection. Here’s what you should know:

1. Right to Safety

  • You have the right to be safe from harm.
  • Law enforcement must act to protect you, and protection or restraining orders can be issued against abusers.

2. Right to Legal Action

  • You can file complaints and take legal action against perpetrators.
  • Fast-track courts and legal processes exist for cases like domestic violence and sexual assault.

3. Right to Confidentiality

  • Your identity and personal information are protected by law.
  • Survivors’ records and reports are kept confidential to prevent stigma and further harm.

4. Right to Medical Care

  • You can access emergency medical services, counseling, and forensic examinations.
  • Medical care and mental health support are your legal rights after any incident of abuse.

5. Right to Support Services

  • Shelters, helplines, NGOs, and government programs are available to help you.
  • Financial assistance and rehabilitation programs may be provided in some cases.

6. Right to Be Heard

  • You have the right to report the abuse and participate in investigations and trials.
  • Laws ensure you are protected from discrimination, intimidation, or victim-blaming.

7. Right to Legal Aid

  • Free legal support is available for survivors who cannot afford it.
  • NGOs and government schemes provide legal counseling, assistance, and guidance.

Remember: You are not alone. Your safety, dignity, and rights are protected by law, and help is always available.
