Our Journey

2025 August — The Idea

It all began with a vision to create a safer environment for women using the power of technology and innovation.

2025 September — The Prototype

Our team developed the first version of SecureHer — an AI-based safety alert and community support platform.

SecureHer partnered with NGOs and government organizations, reaching over 50,000 women through awareness drives and training sessions.

2026 — Future Goals

Expanding globally to make SecureHer an international platform for women’s empowerment, awareness, and safety.
